Teams for Linux

Microsoft Teams with multiple accounts, native notifications
and meeting alerts — in a real desktop app.

Electron Wayland KDE Plasma Arch / pacman

There's no official Teams for Linux.

In the browser: one account at a time, and notifications when they feel like it.

So I built my own.

THE APP

Teams as a real app

Its own window, a tray icon and KDE integration. No more tabs lost in the browser.

01 · MULTI-ACCOUNT

Several accounts, side by side

Each account has its own isolated session, cookies and sign-in. Switching is one click on the corner button.

02 · BELL

Know what landed in your other accounts

The bell lights up with unread messages from the accounts off screen, and the tray icon gets a red dot.

03 · NOTIFICATIONS

Native notifications, with sound

Straight to the system, with the account name. Reminders repeat while there are unread messages — and a click opens the right account.

04 · CALENDAR

Your next meeting, always in sight

Read from every account's calendar and shown in the header, with a countdown.
